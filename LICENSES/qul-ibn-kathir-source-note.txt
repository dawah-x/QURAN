Bengali Tafseer ibn Kathir — provenance / rights note
======================================================

Original work: Tafsir Ibn Kathir (Hafiz Ibn Kathir).
Bengali edition credited to Tawheed Publication.
Digital source: Quranic Universal Library (QUL) by Tarteel AI, resource 31
                https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources/tafsir/31
Verbatim JSON used: spa5k/tafsir_api (MIT-licensed code), path
                tafseer/bn-tafseer-ibn-e-kaseer/<surah>.json
                https://github.com/spa5k/tafsir_api

QUL states that resource licences vary per resource and that some translation /
tafsir texts remain subject to their original publishers' rights. The QUL
resource page for 31 does not publish an explicit redistribution licence, and
spa5k/tafsir_api's MIT licence covers its code, not the tafsir text. MMRBD's
parallel QUL export likewise says: "Original text rights and attribution remain
applicable; this release does not assign a blanket open-source licence to the
tafsir text."

Therefore DAWAH X makes NO open-source licence claim over this Bengali tafsir
text. It is included with visible attribution to Ibn Kathir, Tawheed
Publication, QUL/Tarteel and spa5k/tafsir_api, stored verbatim in
data/tafsir_ibn_kathir_bn.json.gz, and shown only inside the reader alongside
the existing QuranEnc tafsir. On objection from the rights holder the data will
be replaced or removed. The existing QuranEnc `bengali_zakaria` text and its
7 re-publication terms remain untouched.

This file is a provenance note, not a licence grant.
